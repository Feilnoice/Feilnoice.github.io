About Shunran Shen

Character design that supports story, performance, and production.

Shunran is a concept artist focused on character design for animation and games. Her approach combines strong silhouettes, purposeful costume language, and emotionally clear expressions.

Focus Areas

  • Character ideation and exploration
  • Costume and material breakdowns
  • Expression sheets and personality passes
  • Clean turnaround-ready concept sheets

How She Works

  • Starts with story role and emotional tone
  • Builds shape families before detail
  • Tests readability at thumbnail scale
  • Refines for clarity and handoff efficiency

Design Statement

Characters should feel specific, not generic.

Every visual choice should answer who the character is, where they came from, and how they move through their world. This keeps the design grounded and helps teams align quickly across story, art, and production.
