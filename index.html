Workflow

From Spark to Final Sheet

  1. 01 - Discovery

    Mood boards, story hooks, and shape families to set a clear design intent.

  2. 02 - Iteration

    Thumbnail batches focused on proportion rhythm and recognizable silhouettes.

  3. 03 - Refinement

    Costume logic, materials, and color hierarchy tuned for role and readability.

  4. 04 - Delivery

    Final line + paint package with callouts, expressions, and clean turnarounds.
